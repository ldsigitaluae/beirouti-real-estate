# Solara Bay Visual Style Guide

## Brand character
Quiet, assured Dubai property expertise. The visual language combines airy coastal light, precise property data, and restrained editorial luxury.

## Colour system
- Paper — `#FFFFFF`: primary background and clean surfaces
- Stone — `#F0F7FD`: pale-blue section background
- Ink — `#0E2B47`: headlines, primary controls, and high-contrast details
- Body — `#2A4560`: paragraph and navigation text
- Muted — `#4F6780`: supporting copy
- Bay — `#E3F0FB`: feature bands and soft emphasis
- Bay 2 — `#CFE5F7`: image transitions and atmospheric fields
- Tide — `#F4F9FE`: lightest blue wash
- Tide 2 — `#EAF4FC`: footer and secondary bands
- Accent blue — `#8EC5F0`: primary calls to action and highlights
- Accent soft — `#B7DBF7`: icon backgrounds and subtle emphasis
- Accent text — `#1F5F96`: links, focus states, and small labels
- Borders — `#D8E6F2`, stronger `#6F8BA6`

Use white and pale blue as the dominant surfaces. Navy supplies contrast. Accent blue should guide attention rather than fill large areas.

## Typography
- Display: **Bodoni Moda**, regular weight. Use consistently for headings across the website and theme; italic blue highlights remain in the opening headline.
- Body/UI: **Manrope**, weights 400–700. Use for paragraphs, navigation, forms, labels, and controls.
- Headings use compact line-height and sentence case. Body copy remains spacious and direct.

## Layout
- Maximum content width: 1200px.
- Twelve-column editorial grid with generous white space.
- Opening composition: two-line headline and informative copy on the left; a larger bright Dubai Marina photo close to the copy on the right, with square corners and no shadow or overlays.
- The complete opening composition, including its actions and trust line, must fit within one normal-zoom screen at every supported size; never rely on scrolling or zooming out to reveal it.
- Sections are full-width bands, not floating containers. Individual property, service, or proof items may be framed.
- Calls to action use square corners and the same dark-blue style throughout; secondary opening action may use an outlined variant. Editorial frames remain restrained.

## Imagery
- Bright, authentic Dubai architecture and waterfront scenes.
- High-key daylight, pale sky, clean whites, controlled saturation.
- Avoid dark dusk scenes, heavy orange grading, generic business handshakes, or over-staged people.
- Use landscape crops for communities and a portrait/editorial crop for advisory content.

## Iconography and graphics
- Thin outlined icons, 1.25–1.5px strokes, rounded line caps.
- Use navy or accent-text blue, usually on pale-blue or white grounds.
- Skyline, shoreline, and measured line motifs may separate sections, but never compete with content.

## Motion
- Calm reveals: 180–720ms with soft ease-out curves.
- Images enter with a small scale reduction; buttons and arrows shift only a few pixels.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Voice
Clear, factual, and reassuring. Lead with evidence and practical guidance. Avoid hype, urgency tricks, and unverified claims.
