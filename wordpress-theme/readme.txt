== Installation ==
1. Go to Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme.
2. Choose dubai-realestate-v3.0.1.zip, install it and activate it.
3. Open Dashboard > Demo Import (also under Appearance > Demo Import). Preview and select pages and communities, then import. You may retry or re-import at any time.
4. Go to Settings > Permalinks. Choose Post name and save.

== Edit your website ==
Appearance > Customize > dubai realestate Theme:
* Brand: company name, legal name, logo tagline, footer text and copyright.
* Contact details: phone, WhatsApp, email and hours.
* Licences: DED and RERA ORN numbers.
* Opening section: both headline lines, short text, buttons, quote and main image.
* Home page sections: main headings and introductions.
* Navigation: fallback labels and header button.
* Footer: column headings.
* Social links and colours.

Appearance > Menus:
* Assign any menu to Main navigation.
* Assign separate menus to both footer columns.

Site Identity:
* Upload your company logo and browser icon.

All enquiry forms open WhatsApp using your saved WhatsApp number.
